President University Ranked 1st in the World for Entrepreneurial Spirit in WURI 2025

12 Juli 2025 Admin SPMI

President University has achieved another major international milestone in the World University Rankings for Innovation (WURI) 2025, earning 1st place globally in Entrepreneurial Spirit, as well as 2nd place worldwide in Crisis Management and Infrastructure & Technology. In addition, the university was recognized among the Global Top 400 Innovative Universities, ranking 220th in the world, further affirming its global reputation for innovation and societal impact.

These achievements reflect President University’s strength in fostering an educational ecosystem centered on innovation, entrepreneurship, resilience, and digital transformation. Its top global recognition in Entrepreneurial Spirit aligns with the university’s long-standing commitment to embedding entrepreneurship across the student journey through flagship programs such as Economic Survival, SetSail BizAccel, and the innovation ecosystem at Fablab Jababeka. Through this approach, students are prepared not only as job seekers, but also as creators of solutions and future enterprises.

President University’s excellence in Crisis Management also demonstrates the institution’s capacity to develop adaptive education systems in response to global challenges. Innovations such as The BUiLD Disaster Resilience Framework for Higher Education Institutions highlight its commitment to institutional resilience while equipping students with skills to navigate uncertainty. Meanwhile, recognition in Infrastructure & Technology underscores successful integration of technology into education through innovations such as DEMI AI, EduPlay AI, and technology-driven research and learning infrastructure.

President University Rector Handa S. Abidin noted that this achievement reflects the university’s enduring vision of nurturing innovative and resilient future leaders. The WURI 2025 results further reinforce President University’s position as an Indonesian university making meaningful contributions to impactful innovation on the global stage.
