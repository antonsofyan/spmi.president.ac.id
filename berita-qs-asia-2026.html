President University Debuts in QS Asia University Rankings 2026, Strengthening Regional Recognition

24 November 2025 Admin SPMI

President University proudly marks a significant milestone with its first inclusion in the QS Asia University Rankings 2026, securing a position in the 1201–1300 band among 1,526 universities across Asia and ranking 53rd nationally in Indonesia. This achievement places President University within the top 14.9 percent of universities in Asia, reflecting growing recognition of the university’s quality, international engagement, and institutional development.

The ranking highlights several of President University’s notable strengths, particularly in internationalization and student mobility. Strong performance in the International Students Ratio and Outbound Exchange Students Ratio demonstrates the university’s continued commitment to fostering a globally connected academic environment. The report also notes positive progress in staff qualifications, with a significant proportion of academic staff holding doctoral degrees, reinforcing the institution’s dedication to academic quality and research capacity.

As its first participation in the QS Asia rankings, this achievement represents an important foundation for future growth. The report also identifies strategic opportunities for improvement, particularly in enhancing research productivity, academic reputation, and faculty resources. Moving forward, President University aims to strengthen international research collaboration, expand global partnerships, and further improve teaching and research excellence as part of its long-term strategy to advance in future regional and global rankings.
