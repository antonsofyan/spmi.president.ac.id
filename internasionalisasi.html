Mobility Programs & International Office Services

Official services of President University's International Office facilitating international student needs, global mobility programs, and exchange scholarships.

Student Mobility & Scholarships

  • Student Mobility Program (Inbound & Outbound): Facilitates student and lecturer exchanges with foreign partner universities and incoming international students at President University.
  • Mobility Scholarships: Support through scholarships like DAAD (EMPM program), Indonesian Aid Scholarship (TIAS), Southeast Asia Leadership Program, and CMK Hyundai.
  • Visa & Immigration Support: Comprehensive guidance for visa application, residence permit extension, and immigration regulation compliance.

Integration & Cultural Events

  • President University Buddy Program: Pairing international students with local students for language and cultural exchange.
  • Cultural Festival & International Dinners: Annual events showcasing cultures and international cuisine.
  • Student Services: Academic counseling, social support, language assistance, and cultural adaptation services.
